Privacy Policy

Pixel Sudoku - Sudoku Puzzle Game

Last updated: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixel Sudoku (the "App") collects, uses, and protects your personal information. On first launch, the App asks for your consent to this policy before starting any feature that involves personal data (sign-in and ads).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account & Payment Information (sent to server)

Type Items Purpose
Account identifier Firebase user ID (UID) issued at Google sign-in Linking the ad-removal entitlement and verifying purchases
Payment information Google Play purchase token, order ID, product ID Validating in-app purchases and preventing duplicate grants

Your email address and display name may be provided during Google sign-in, but the App does not store them and discards them immediately.

1.2 Advertising & Analytics Information (collected automatically)

  • Advertising identifier & device information: The Google AdMob SDK collects and uses the advertising identifier (AAID) and device information to serve ads. Depending on your consent to personalized ads, either personalized or non-personalized ads are shown.
  • Usage statistics (anonymous analytics): To improve the service, we collect gameplay usage statistics — such as stage progress, clears/failures, feature usage, and ticket flow — via Google Analytics. A random, non-identifying analytics identifier is used, and collection occurs only after you accept the privacy consent.

1.3 Information Stored Only on Your Device (never sent to server)

  • Profile: Nickname you enter and the avatar you select
  • Game data: Stage progress, records/statistics, currency such as tickets, and app settings

This information is stored only in your device's local storage and is not transmitted externally.

What we do NOT collect: The App does not use crash-reporting tools, and does not access your location, contacts, photos, or other personal files on your device. Usage statistics are collected only in anonymous, non-identifying form.

2. App Permissions

Permission Purpose Required
Internet (INTERNET) Sign-in, purchase verification, ad delivery Required
Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) Checking network connectivity Required

3. How We Use Information

  • Verifying in-app purchases and managing the ad-removal entitlement
  • Identifying your account (keeping the ad-removal entitlement across devices)
  • Serving ads and operating the service

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Google services.

4.1 Firebase (Google)

  • Firebase Authentication: User authentication via Google sign-in
  • Cloud Functions / Cloud Firestore: Purchase verification, ad-removal entitlement record, and cloud save (game progress) storage
  • Google Analytics: Anonymous gameplay usage analytics (for service improvement)

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4.2 Google AdMob (Ads)

The App serves banner and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. The advertising identifier and related data are used in the ad-serving process.

Google Ads policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4.3 Google Sign-In & Google Play Billing

  • Google Sign-In: Google Privacy Policy
  • In-app purchases: Payments are processed by Google Play, and payment method details (e.g., cards) are managed by Google Play. The App does not collect payment method information.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Local storage: Profile and game data are stored on your device.
  • Cloud storage: Purchase records and the ad-removal entitlement are stored on Firebase servers (Google Cloud Platform).
  • All network communication is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Firebase security rules restrict access so that only your own account's data can be accessed.

6. Data Retention

  • Purchase/entitlement records: Retained for the period necessary to operate the service and handle payment disputes.
  • Upon account deletion request: Related records stored on the server are deleted within 30 days of the request.
  • Local data: Deleted immediately by uninstalling the App or using "Reset Data" in the App.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights.

  • Access: Request to view your personal information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent to data processing and to personalized ads
How to delete your data:
  1. On-device data: App settings → Reset Data
  2. Server records (purchase/entitlement): Request deletion at the contact below, including your account identifier

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 14 and older and provides an age-confirmation and consent step on first launch. Children under 14 require the consent of a legal guardian. The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children under 14, and will delete such information immediately if it becomes aware of it.

9. Advertising

The App serves ads through Google AdMob. On the first-launch consent screen, you can choose whether to allow personalized ads; if you do not consent, non-personalized ads are shown. Purchasing ad removal via in-app purchase hides banner ads.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised in accordance with legal or service changes. Significant changes will be announced within the App or on this page.
